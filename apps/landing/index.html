What it does

Everything between "I'd like a coffee" and "number 42 is ready".

The pieces a food business actually needs, working from the same menu and the same prices.

Counter and kitchen screens

A quick till, a kitchen screen that shows an order the moment it is paid, and a queue display for the wall. Tap Start, tap Ready, and everyone sees it.

Customers order from their phone

Scan a table's QR code and order with photos, hot or cold, extras and live status. Orders paid with member credit go straight to the kitchen; every other order is checked and accepted by staff first. Customers can switch the page to English, Malay, Indonesian, Mandarin, Japanese, French, German or Tamil.

Keeps working offline

Every order is saved on the device first and sent when the connection returns. A network drop during lunch is an inconvenience, not an outage.

Prepaid credit

Load credit at the counter or upload a member list with starting balances. Members sign in with their phone and pay from their balance. Each spend is checked against one balance, so two tills cannot spend the same dollars, and every movement is recorded, with a history for each member showing who added or spent what.

One menu, a price for each way you sell

Dine in, take away, delivery partners or an event can each have their own price list and service charge, set once and applied everywhere.

Receipts, tickets and labels

Prints to receipt printers, separate kitchen and drinks printers, and Zebra label printers, with Chinese and English on the same ticket. If a printer is off, the job waits and retries instead of disappearing, and a manager can see what failed.

How an order flows

From a phone to a printed ticket in five steps.

The same path works for orders a customer places and orders your staff key in.

  1. Customer ordersPicks items and options on their phone and gets a queue number.
  2. Staff acceptThe order appears on the counter. One tap accepts it, or turns it down. Orders paid with credit are accepted automatically.
  3. PaymentCash, or from the customer's prepaid credit. The kitchen starts once it is paid.
  4. Kitchen ticketShown on the kitchen screen and printed, with every choice on it.
  5. ReadyThe queue display and the customer's phone both say so.

In control

Staff, devices and money you can account for.

  • Staff sign in with a PIN, and roles decide who can refund or change prices.
  • A new tablet or printer hub has to be approved with a one-time code, and a lost one can be removed in a tap.
  • An audit trail shows who changed a price, topped up credit or approved a device.
  • Managers change prices, photos and the shop's look themselves, without calling anyone.
