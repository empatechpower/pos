Solutions

One platform of connected software solutions — plus the infrastructure services behind them. Rezein is a platform, not an ERP: it connects with the accounting and third-party software your business already uses.

Software Solutions

Online Ordering System

Your own branded online ordering channel, connected directly to your point of sale and operations. Orders flow into the same workflow as walk-in sales, so there is no separate system to reconcile at the end of the day.

Self-Service Kiosk

Self-service ordering kiosks that shorten queues, present your full menu clearly, and free your staff to focus on preparation and service during peak periods.

Point of Sale System

A fast, reliable point of sale built for quick service, table service and retail. It supports multi-channel pricing and the payment methods your customers expect — PayNow direct to your own bank account, and cards via partner gateways.

Procurement System

Ordering, goods receiving and supplier management connected to your stock and sales, so purchasing reflects what your business actually sells and uses.

Workforce Optimisation System

Rostering and workforce tools that help managers plan the right staffing levels for peak and off-peak periods, and keep labour cost under control.

Analytics & Data Mining

Sales, product and customer analytics drawn from your own trading data — clear reporting that helps owners and managers decide what to promote, what to stock and where to improve.

Integrations

Rezein integrates with accounting software and third-party software, so data flows between the systems your business already relies on. Integrations include delivery platforms such as GrabFood and Foodpanda.

Infrastructure Services

Rezein covers the full in-store technology stack, not just software. Our platform depends on a reliable network and secure premises, so we provide the infrastructure services that support it — planned, installed and supported together with your software, from a single provider.

CCTV Surveillance Systems

Camera systems for security, loss prevention and operational visibility across your premises, including:

  • 5-megapixel cameras with full-colour night vision
  • Multi-channel network video recorders (NVR) supporting multiple hard drives

Wi-Fi Networks

Reliable in-store Wi-Fi for your systems, your staff and your customers.

Broadband Connectivity

Business broadband connectivity arranged and supported for your outlets.

Firewall & Network Security

Firewall configuration and network security that protect your systems and your customers’ data.

VPN

Secure VPN connections between outlets and head office, and for authorised remote access.

Server Setup & Support

Server setup and ongoing support for the systems your operations depend on, including:

  • Virtualisation
  • Disaster recovery
  • High-availability setup

Supported Hardware

Rezein is hardware-agnostic. It runs on a wide range of existing hardware, so there is no forced hardware bundle and no need to replace equipment that still works. The devices below are examples of hardware Rezein runs on — not a required purchase list.

  • Dual-screen Android POS terminals
  • iPad
  • Android tablets
  • Sunmi handheld terminals
  • Sunmi kiosk terminals
  • Castor kiosk
  • Zebra label printers
  • Epson receipt printers
  • Rongta receipt printers

If your business already owns terminals, tablets or printers, talk to us first — in most cases they can be recycled and kept in service with Rezein.
