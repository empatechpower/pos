About Us

Rezein Pte Ltd is a Singapore technology company providing a connected platform for customer-facing businesses.

What We Do

Rezein provides one connected platform covering point of sale, online ordering, self-service kiosks, procurement, workforce optimisation, analytics and data mining, together with integration to accounting software and third-party software. Alongside the software, our infrastructure services — CCTV surveillance, Wi-Fi, broadband connectivity, network security, VPN and servers — cover the full in-store technology stack.

Rezein is a platform, not an ERP. We focus on the customer-facing and in-store operations of a business, and connect to the other systems a business relies on, rather than replacing them.

How We Work

AI-powered operations. We use AI agents extensively to automate routine support and change requests. This allows us to support our customers responsively with a lean team, with human escalation whenever a matter needs it.

Hardware-agnostic. Our software is platform-agnostic and runs on a wide range of existing hardware. Businesses do not need to buy hardware from us; they can recycle the terminals, tablets and printers they already own.

Direct, low-cost payments. PayNow payments through Rezein go direct to the merchant’s own bank account, at the lowest cost in the market. Card payments are accepted via partner gateways.
