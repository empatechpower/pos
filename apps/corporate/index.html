About Rezein

A connected platform for businesses that serve customers every day

Rezein Pte. Ltd. is a Singapore technology company. We provide a single operating platform for restaurants, retailers and service businesses — covering how customers order and pay, how stock is bought and received, how teams are scheduled, and how owners see performance. Alongside the software, we supply and support the in-store infrastructure it runs on, so one partner is accountable for the whole setup.

Common questions

Answers, before you ask

Is Rezein an ERP system?
No. Rezein is a commerce and operations platform covering ordering, POS, procurement, workforce and analytics. It integrates with accounting software and other third-party systems rather than replacing them as an ERP would.
Do we need to buy new hardware to use Rezein?
In most cases, no. Rezein is hardware-agnostic and runs on a wide range of existing equipment — including dual-screen Android POS terminals, iPad and Android tablets, Castor and Sunmi kiosks, Sunmi handhelds, and Zebra, Epson and Rongta printers. We confirm reuse for your exact models during the initial assessment.
What does the PayNow lowest-cost commitment cover?
It covers PayNow acceptance, where payment is made direct to the merchant's own bank account. We offer PayNow at the lowest cost a merchant can find in the market. Card payments are processed through payment gateways; their rates are set by the gateway, so Rezein does not guarantee card rates.
Can Rezein look after our store's CCTV and network as well?
Yes. Our infrastructure services cover CCTV surveillance (5-megapixel cameras with full-colour night vision and multi-channel NVRs supporting multiple hard drives), Wi-Fi, broadband, firewall and network security, VPN, and servers — including virtualisation, disaster recovery and high-availability setups.
Which delivery platforms does Rezein integrate with?
Rezein integrates with GrabFood and Foodpanda, alongside accounting software and other third-party systems, so delivery orders and financial data flow into the same platform as in-store sales.
