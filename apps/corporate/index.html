Why Businesses Choose Rezein

AI-Powered Support & Change Requests

Rezein uses AI agents extensively to handle routine support and change requests. That keeps response times fast and operations lean — without the need for a large support team — while human specialists step in whenever a request needs escalation.

Hardware-Agnostic

There is no forced hardware purchase. Rezein runs on the hardware your business already owns, so existing terminals, tablets and printers can be recycled and kept in service instead of being replaced.

PayNow Direct, at the Lowest Cost

PayNow payments go direct to the merchant’s own bank account, at the lowest cost in the market. Card payments are accepted via partner gateways, so merchants keep a choice of providers.
