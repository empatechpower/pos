Last updated: 19 November 2026

Rezein Pte Ltd (“Rezein”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal data collected through this website, in line with the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

What We Collect

When you contact us through this website or by phone, we may collect your name, company name, contact number, email address (if you provide one) and the contents of your enquiry. This website does not operate user accounts and does not process payments.

How We Use It

We use the information you provide only to respond to your enquiry, provide information about our solutions and services, and manage our business relationship with you. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Disclosure

We may share personal data with service providers who support our operations, only as needed for them to perform their services, or where required by law or a competent authority.

Retention and Security

We retain enquiry records for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes above and take reasonable steps to protect personal data against unauthorised access, collection, use or disclosure.
