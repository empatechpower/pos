Industries

Rezein serves customer-facing businesses across food & beverage, retail, beauty and wellness, entertainment and logistics — on one connected platform.

QSR

Quick service restaurants need speed and accuracy at every channel. Rezein supports counter point of sale, self-service kiosks and online ordering on one system, with a queue-friendly workflow built for peak hours.

Table Service Restaurants

Full-service restaurants can manage tables, orders and billing on one platform, with channel pricing and payment options that suit dine-in service.

Retail & Grocery

Retailers, mini-marts and grocery stores use Rezein for point of sale, procurement and goods receiving, keeping stock and supplier records connected to daily sales.

Beauty Salons

Beauty salons use Rezein to manage services, sales and repeat customers, with the reporting to see which services and packages perform.

Spa & Services

Spa and service businesses run on bookings, packages and returning customers. Rezein gives them one system for sales and customer records.

Entertainment

Entertainment venues handle high-volume, time-sensitive sales. Rezein provides a fast point of sale and clear reporting across busy trading periods.

Logistics

Logistics businesses use Rezein’s connected platform and infrastructure services to keep operations, sites and teams linked.
